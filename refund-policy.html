Refund Policy

How FastCorr handles credit restorations, Premium cancellations, and cash refund requests for organisation wallets and lawyer seats.

Last updated: 27 July 2026 Applies to credit purchases, Premium subscriptions, and job checkout

1. Overview

FastCorr distinguishes between two kinds of redress. Organisation credit restoration for cancelled jobs is the primary refund path. Cash refunds of credit top-ups or Premium fees are not automated and are handled only at FastCorr’s discretion (see section 5).

Type What it means
Credit restoration Credits returned to the organisation wallet after an approved job cancellation or a failed checkout
Cash / card refund Money returned to the original payment method (Paystack / card / bank)

2. Credits used on jobs

When you check out a litigation request or messenger order, the full job cost (including applicable fees, surcharges, and VAT) is deducted from your organisation’s credit balance.

2.1 Requesting a cancellation

A lawyer may request cancellation when the job is still eligible:

Flow Lawyer may request cancel when status is…
Litigation Pending, Assigned, or In progress
Messenger Pending only

Once work has progressed further — for example litigation reaches ready for pickup, or a messenger job is accepted by a driver — lawyers cannot self-cancel. Office administrators may still intervene.

When a lawyer requests cancellation:

  1. The job moves to Cancellation pending review
  2. The organisation’s office reviews the request
  3. Credits are not restored until the office approves the cancellation

While review is pending, the lawyer may withdraw the cancellation request. Withdrawing restores the previous job status and does not change the credit balance (credits were never restored).

2.2 Office review outcomes

Decision Effect on the job Effect on credits
Approve Job is cancelled Full credit restoration of the stored job cost
Reverse Job returns to its previous status and continues No credit change
Deny Cancellation is not approved No credit restoration

Approved restorations are recorded on the organisation’s credit transaction ledger as a cancellation refund.

2.3 What is restored

  • Full organisation credits equal to the job’s stored cost at cancellation time
  • Restorations are credits only — not a cash refund to a card or bank account
  • There is no automatic prorated credit restoration based on job progress; the office decides whether to approve a full restoration

2.4 Failed checkout

If credits are deducted but checkout fails:

  • Litigation: the system attempts an automatic credit rollback
  • Messenger / delivery: automatic rollback may not apply — contact FastCorr support with your transaction reference so the balance can be corrected

3. Credit top-ups

Credit packages and custom top-ups (minimum R1,000) are prepaid value loaded into the organisation wallet.

  • Successful top-ups add credits to the organisation; they are not tied to a single job
  • Unused organisation credits remain available for future jobs
  • Automatic cash refunds of unused credit top-ups are not provided through the app
  • If a payment is charged in error (duplicate charge, failed fulfilment after payment, or similar), contact FastCorr support promptly with the Paystack / payment reference

Card verification holds (if used when adding a payment method) are described in-product as small confirmation charges that are refunded; any such hold follows the payment provider’s normal verification process.

4. Premium subscriptions

Premium is billed per lawyer seat (R500 / month or R5,000 / year). Premium is separate from organisation credits.

Cancellation

  • You may cancel Premium at any time via Paystack / Payment settings
  • After cancellation, Premium access continues until the end of the current paid billing period
  • You may resubscribe later without losing your existing case data

Refunds on Premium

  • No prorated cash refunds are offered for unused time in a billing period
  • Cancelling mid-cycle does not reverse the current period’s charge
  • Payment failures may enter a short grace period during which access can continue; after grace expires without successful payment, the seat returns to the Free tier

5. Cash refunds (Paystack / card)

FastCorr does not automatically issue cash or card refunds for:

  • Cancelled litigation or messenger jobs (those use credit restoration when approved)
  • Unused organisation credit balances
  • Unused portions of Premium subscriptions

Cash refunds may be considered manually only in exceptional cases, for example:

  • Duplicate or erroneous charges
  • Payment taken where credits or Premium access were never delivered
  • Other billing errors confirmed by FastCorr

To request a review, email info@fastcorr.co.za or danny@fastcorr.co.za with:

  • Organisation name
  • User / lawyer email
  • Payment or transaction reference
  • Job / order ID (if applicable)
  • Brief description of the issue

6. Non-refundable situations

Unless FastCorr agrees otherwise in writing, the following are not eligible for credit restoration or cash refund:

Situation Reason
Job completed successfully Service was delivered
Lawyer cancel after eligibility window Work has progressed past self-cancel stages
Cancellation denied or reversed by the office Credits remain applied to the active / unresolved job
Admin force-cancellation without approved credit rollback Automatic credit restoration is not guaranteed
Unused credit balance in the wallet Credits are prepaid service value, not cash on demand
Mid-cycle Premium cancellation Access continues to period end; no prorated refund
Change of mind after a successful credit top-up Prepaid credits remain in the organisation wallet

7. How to request a cancellation or billing review

Job cancellation (credit restoration path)

  1. Open the request or order in the FastCorr User app
  2. Choose Cancel (when available) and provide a short reason if prompted
  3. Wait for your office to Approve, Reverse, or Deny the request
  4. If approved, confirm that organisation credits have been restored under Payment / credit history

Billing or payment dispute

Contact FastCorr support with the details listed in section 5.

8. Policy summary

Scenario Typical outcome
Eligible job cancelled and approved by office Full credits restored to the organisation
Job cancellation reversed or denied No credit restoration
Checkout fails after deduct (litigation) Automatic credit rollback
Unused credit wallet balance No automatic cash refund
Premium cancelled mid-period Access until period end; no prorated cash refund
Duplicate / failed payment Manual review via support
